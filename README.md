# 7 Deadly Sins Test

A free browser quiz with 28 statements covering pride, greed, lust, envy, gluttony, wrath, and sloth. The quiz takes about four minutes and you can start without an account.

[Take the 7 Deadly Sins Test](https://7-deadly-sins-test.org/?utm_source=github.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=github_project&utm_content=description_link)

## How to take the quiz

1. Rate how much you agree with each statement on a five-point scale.
2. See your scores for all seven sins and the virtue paired with your highest score.
3. Choose **Share result**. When a friend opens your link and takes the quiz, you can compare both results on one chart.

Your answers stay in your browser. Shared links carry your seven scores.

## Example

If wrath is your highest score, the quiz pairs it with patience. Think of a recent conversation where you felt rushed to respond, and consider what taking a short pause would have changed.
